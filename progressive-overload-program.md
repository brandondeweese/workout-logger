# 12-Week Progressive Overload Program
**4 lifting days/week (legs 1x/week) + separate running day | 25–30 working sets/session**

---

## Structure Overview

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Upper — Push-dominant + Abs |
| Day 2 | Upper — Pull-dominant + Abs |
| Day 3 | Legs (full lower body — only leg day of the week) + Abs |
| Day 4 | Upper — Mixed/full upper + Abs |
| + | 3-mile run on a separate day, not counted in lifting volume |

Three phases, each building on the last. Deload after each phase.

- **Phase 1 — Accumulation** (Weeks 1–4): Volume/hypertrophy emphasis
- **Phase 2 — Intensification** (Weeks 5–8): Strength emphasis
- **Phase 3 — Realization** (Weeks 9–11): Peak intensity, lower volume
- **Week 12 — Deload/Test**: Drop volume ~50%, retest key lifts

Since legs only get one session, that day carries full lower-body coverage (squat + hinge + accessories) every week rather than alternating patterns like a typical 2-day lower split would. Abs are spread across all four days rather than stacked on leg day, so core work doesn't get crowded out and recovers between hits.

**Squat + Leg Press same day, on purpose:** Squat is the primary quad/whole-leg driver; leg press is added quad volume that doesn't add spinal loading or extra systemic fatigue the way a second squat session would. Since legs are trained only once a week here, stacking them is how you get enough quad volume without needing a second leg day. Squat goes first while you're fresh; leg press is the fatigue-tolerant back-off work. If leg press feels redundant or beats you up too much for the run, swap it for hack squat, walking lunge, or just drop it and add a set to RDL/leg curl instead.

## Phase 1: Accumulation (Weeks 1–4)
**Load: 65–75% 1RM | Reps: 8–12 | RIR: 2–3 | Rest: 60–90s**

### Day 1 – Upper Push
- Barbell Bench Press: 4×8 (RIR 3)
- Overhead Press: 4×10
- Incline DB Press: 3×10
- DB Lateral Raise: 3×15
- Cable Fly (slow tempo, 3-1-3): 3×12
- Triceps Pushdown: 3×12
- Overhead Triceps Extension: 3×12
- Face Pull: 3×15
- Cable Crunch: 3×15

*Total: 29 sets*

### Day 2 – Upper Pull
- Weighted Pull-up: 4×8
- Pendlay Row: 4×10
- Chest-supported Row: 4×10
- Lat Pulldown: 3×12
- Seated Cable Row: 3×12
- DB Rear Delt Fly: 3×15
- EZ Bar Curl: 3×12
- Hammer Curl: 3×12
- Hanging Leg Raise: 3×15

*Total: 30 sets*

### Day 3 – Legs (full lower body)
- Back Squat: 4×8 (RIR 3)
- Leg Press: 3×12
- Romanian Deadlift: 4×10
- Walking Lunge: 3×12/leg
- Leg Extension: 3×15
- Seated Leg Curl: 3×15
- Standing Calf Raise: 4×15
- Seated Calf Raise: 3×15
- Weighted Plank: 3×45s

*Total: 30 sets*

### Day 4 – Upper Mixed
- Incline Bench Press (pause reps, 2-count): 4×10
- DB Overhead Press: 3×10
- Seated Cable Row: 4×10
- Lat Pulldown (wide grip): 3×12
- DB Lateral Raise: 3×15
- Cable Fly: 2×12
- EZ Bar Curl: 3×12
- Triceps Pushdown: 3×12
- Ab Wheel Rollout: 3×12

*Total: 29 sets*

## Phase 2: Intensification (Weeks 5–8)
**Load: 75–85% 1RM | Reps: 4–8 | RIR: 1–2 | Rest: 2–3 min**

Cut isolation volume ~20%, add a top-set/back-off structure on main lifts.

### Day 1 – Upper Push
- Barbell Bench Press: Top set 3-5 reps @ RIR 1, then 3×6 @ 85% of top set
- Overhead Press: 3×6
- Incline DB Press: 3×8
- DB Lateral Raise: 2×15
- Cable Fly: 2×12
- Triceps Pushdown: 3×10
- Face Pull: 2×15
- Cable Crunch: 2×15

*Total: 24 sets*

### Day 2 – Upper Pull
- Weighted Pull-up: 4×6
- Pendlay Row: 4×8
- Chest-supported Row: 3×8
- Lat Pulldown: 3×10
- Seated Cable Row: 2×10
- EZ Bar Curl: 2×10
- Hammer Curl: 2×10
- Hanging Leg Raise: 2×15

*Total: 24 sets*

### Day 3 – Legs
- Back Squat: Top set 3-5 reps, then 3×6 @ 85%
- Leg Press: 3×10
- Romanian Deadlift: 3×8
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 3×8/leg
- Leg Extension: 2×15
- Seated Leg Curl: 3×12
- Standing Calf Raise: 3×12
- Weighted Plank: 2×45s

*Total: 24 sets → add a set of leg press or leg curl to hit 25*

### Day 4 – Upper Mixed
- Incline Bench Press (pause reps): 3×8
- DB Overhead Press: 3×8
- Seated Cable Row: 4×8
- Lat Pulldown: 3×10
- DB Lateral Raise: 2×15
- EZ Bar Curl: 2×10
- Triceps Pushdown: 3×10
- Ab Wheel Rollout: 2×12

*Total: 26 sets*

## Phase 3: Realization/Peak (Weeks 9–11)
**Load: 85–93% 1RM | Reps: 1–5 | RIR: 0–1 | Rest: 3+ min**

Volume drops intentionally — this phase is about expressing strength, not building it. Isolation and core work become maintenance-only.

### Day 1 – Upper Push
- Barbell Bench Press: 5×3 @ 85-90%
- Close-Grip Bench or Board Press: 3×5
- Overhead Press: 3×5
- Cable Fly: 2×12
- Triceps Pushdown: 2×12
- Cable Crunch: 2×15

*Total: 17 sets*

### Day 2 – Upper Pull
- Weighted Pull-up: 5×3
- Pendlay Row: 3×5
- Chest-supported Row: 2×8
- Lat Pulldown: 2×10
- EZ Bar Curl: 2×10
- Hanging Leg Raise: 2×12

*Total: 16 sets*

### Day 3 – Legs
- Back Squat: 5×3 @ 85-90%
- Leg Press: 2×10
- Romanian Deadlift: 3×6
- Bulgarian Split Squat: 2×8/leg
- Leg Curl: 2×10
- Calf Raise: 2×12
- Weighted Plank: 2×45s

*Total: 15 sets*

### Day 4 – Upper Mixed
- Incline Bench Press (pause reps): 3×5
- DB Overhead Press: 3×6
- Seated Cable Row: 3×6
- Lat Pulldown: 2×10
- Triceps Pushdown: 2×12
- Ab Wheel Rollout: 2×10

*Total: 15 sets*

---

## Week 12: Deload / Retest
- Cut volume to ~12-15 sets/day, load to 50-60% 1RM, RIR 4-5
- Days 1, 2, 4: light technical work across all main lifts, one light ab exercise
- Day 3: Test 1-3RM on Squat, Bench, Deadlift, OHP (or estimate from a hard triple)

---

## Progression Rules
1. **Within a phase**: add 2.5-5 lbs (upper) / 5-10 lbs (lower) per week on main lifts once you hit the top of the rep range at target RIR.
2. **If you miss reps two sessions in a row**: hold load, don't push forward.
3. **Between phases**: main lift weights carry over based on the top-set performance in week 4/8 — recalculate working percentages off your best top set, not an old 1RM.
4. **Legs get one shot a week**: squat first while fresh, leg press second as back-off volume, then hinge pattern. Keep RDL/hinge loads a notch conservative so hamstring/lower-back fatigue doesn't bleed into your 3-mile run.
5. **Chest/bench work**: keep the tempo cue (2-3 count eccentric, deliberate pause at chest) on all incline and pause-rep bench variations through every phase — don't let those slide into speed reps as intensity increases. If chest engagement is inconsistent on a given session, drop load 10% rather than pushing through with poor activation.
6. **Running**: treat the run as its own recovery-sensitive session — schedule it with at least a day of separation from leg day where possible, since squat/RDL soreness will affect pace.
7. **Abs**: rotate the ab exercise (crunch variation, hanging raise, ab wheel, plank) so you're not doing the identical movement twice in a week — this is baked into the days above.

---

## Notes
- Sets listed are working sets only; add a couple warm-up ramp sets on compounds, not counted in the 25-30 target.
- Swap accessory lifts freely for equivalent movement patterns based on equipment access or joint feel — the compound lifts and their progression scheme are the load-bearing structure of this program.
